# Holy Cities of Israel Railway

A playful 3D toy-table railway that runs through the four holy cities of the Land of Israel — **Jerusalem, Hebron, Safed (Tzfat), and Tiberias** — with eight miniature landmarks, the Sea of Galilee, the Jordan River, festival shows from Pesach through Havdalah, and a working train-driver POV with cab controls.

## The Four Holy Cities

- **Jerusalem (Yerushalayim)** — *the Western Wall (Kotel)* on the Temple Mount plaza, and *Migdal David* (the Tower of David citadel) by Jaffa Gate.
- **Hebron (Chevron)** — *Me'arat HaMachpelah* (the Cave of the Patriarchs), the Herodian fortress over the burial cave of Avraham, Sarah, Yitzchak, Rivkah, Yaakov, and Leah; and the *Avraham Avinu Synagogue*.
- **Safed (Tzfat)** — *the Ari HaKadosh Synagogue* with its iconic Safed-blue doors and shutters, and the *Beit Yosef Caro Synagogue* of the author of the Shulchan Aruch. The mystical center of Kabbalah, set high in the Galilee mountains.
- **Tiberias (Teveriya)** — *the Tomb of the Rambam* (Maimonides) with its dramatic red pillars, and the *Tomb of Rabbi Akiva* — both on the shore of Yam Kinneret (the Sea of Galilee).

## Festival Shows

Cycle through six lighting and atmosphere modes drawn from the Jewish year:

- **Pesach** — almond-blossom spring, fresh barley, soft blue sky
- **Shavuot** — golden wheat harvest, early-summer warmth
- **Sukkot** — autumn pomegranates, etrog yellow, harvest light
- **Chanukah** — the festival of lights, with a giant nine-branched chanukiah glowing and snow on Safed's mountains
- **Shabbat** — golden Friday-evening light over the Galilee
- **Havdalah** — Saturday-night sky with stars and Magen David glow

An **Auto tour** mode cycles the festivals automatically with synchronized train-route variations (tunnel through the Judean hills, flyover above the Galilee).

## Symbology

Exclusively Jewish iconography throughout — Magen David finials on every dome, gold mezuzot on doorposts, the Knesset Menorah monument, an Israeli flag plaza, a tzedakah box on each city square, Egged buses, sherut taxis, cypress and olive trees, almond and pomegranate orchards, and the dramatic Sea of Galilee.

## Play It

Open this folder through any simple local web server, then visit the local page in your browser. The project is a creative demo, designed to feel like a polished interactive toy railway rather than a literal map.

URL parameters:
- `?mode=` accepts `pesach` / `shavuot` / `sukkot` / `chanukah` / `shabbat` / `havdalah` (aliases: `hanukkah`, `passover`, `tabernacles`, `sabbath`)
- `?camera=` accepts `jerusalem` / `hebron` / `safed` / `tiberias` / `fullTable`
